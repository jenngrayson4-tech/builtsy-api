About Me

Here to walk beside you — not ahead of you

Licensed LMFT CBT Certified Trauma-Informed 10+ Years 500+ Clients

I'm a licensed therapist who has spent over a decade helping individuals, couples, and groups navigate life's most challenging chapters. My approach is warm, evidence-based, and always centered on your unique story.

I believe that healing isn't linear — and that the bravest thing you can do is ask for support. In our work together, you'll find a safe, non-judgmental space to explore what's holding you back and start building the life you genuinely want.

Whether you're dealing with anxiety, depression, relationship challenges, or a major life transition, I'm here to help you find your footing — one session at a time.
