================================================================
THE WEBSITE BLUEPRINT — AUDIT TOOLS REFERENCE
================================================================

These are the free tools you use to verify every website you
build is performing correctly — for Google, for AI search, for
speed, and for accessibility. Run all of these before you
deliver any site to a client, or after launching your own.

================================================================
1. SCHEMA & STRUCTURED DATA
================================================================

GOOGLE RICH RESULTS TEST
URL: search.google.com/test/rich-results
Cost: Free
What it does: Checks your JSON-LD schema markup. Shows every
structured data type Google detects on your page, flags any
errors or warnings, and confirms eligibility for rich results
in Google Search.
When to use: After every site build, before delivery.
What you want to see: Green checkmarks, zero errors. Non-critical
warnings (orange dots) are acceptable — they usually mean a field
like phone number or address was intentionally left blank.

----------------------------------------------------------------

================================================================
2. AI CRAWLABILITY & ACCESS
================================================================

OTTERLY.AI — CRAWLABILITY AUDIT
URL: otterly.ai
Cost: Free tier available
What it does: Checks whether AI search engines can actually
access and crawl your site. Verifies both server-level firewall
permissions AND your robots.txt settings for every major AI
crawler including:
  - GPTBot (ChatGPT)
  - OAI-SearchBot (ChatGPT Search)
  - ClaudeBot (Anthropic/Claude)
  - PerplexityBot
  - GoogleOther / Google-Extended
  - anthropic-ai
  - GrokBot (X/Twitter)
  - Meta-ExternalAgent / Meta-ExternalFetcher
  - Cohere-ai
  - Diffbot
  - Bytespider
  - CCBot
  - Applebot-Extended
  - ImagesiftBot
  - FacebookBot
  - Omgilibot
When to use: After every site build, before delivery.
What you want to see: "Allow" in green for every single crawler
on both the Server Access Check AND the robots.txt Check.
If anything shows "Block" — fix your robots.txt immediately.

----------------------------------------------------------------

================================================================
3. PAGE SPEED & PERFORMANCE
================================================================

GOOGLE PAGESPEED INSIGHTS
URL: pagespeed.web.dev
Cost: Free
What it does: Scores your site on speed, performance, and
Core Web Vitals for both mobile and desktop. Identifies exactly
what is slowing the site down and how to fix it.
When to use: After every site build, before delivery.
What you want to see: 90+ score on both mobile and desktop.
Images are almost always the cause of low scores — compress
them at squoosh.app and retest.

----------------------------------------------------------------

================================================================
4. ACCESSIBILITY
================================================================

WAVE ACCESSIBILITY CHECKER
URL: wave.webaim.org
Cost: Free
What it does: Audits your site for WCAG 2.1 accessibility
compliance. Flags missing alt text, low color contrast, missing
form labels, keyboard navigation issues, and more.
When to use: After every site build, before delivery.
What you want to see: Zero errors. Alerts and warnings can be
reviewed case by case — errors must be fixed.

----------------------------------------------------------------

================================================================
5. SEO & OVERALL SITE HEALTH
================================================================

GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE
URL: search.google.com/search-console
Cost: Free
What it does: Shows how Google sees and indexes your site.
Submit your sitemap here, monitor for crawl errors, see which
keywords your site appears for, and track indexing status.
When to use: Set up for every site at launch. Check back after
a few weeks to confirm Google has indexed all pages.
What you want to see: All pages indexed, no coverage errors,
sitemap submitted and accepted.

AHREFS FREE WEBMASTER TOOLS
URL: ahrefs.com/webmaster-tools
Cost: Free with site verification
What it does: Full site audit — broken links, missing meta
tags, duplicate content, page speed issues, and more. One of
the most comprehensive free SEO audit tools available.
When to use: After launch, or when troubleshooting SEO issues.
What you want to see: Low health score issues, no broken links,
all pages crawlable.

----------------------------------------------------------------

================================================================
6. AI SEARCH VISIBILITY
================================================================

PERPLEXITY.AI — MANUAL CHECK
URL: perplexity.ai
Cost: Free
What it does: Directly tests whether AI search knows the
business exists. Search for the business name, the business
type + city, and questions customers would actually ask.
When to use: 2–4 weeks after launch (AI tools need time to
index new sites). Also great as a sales tool — show a prospect
they're invisible on AI search before you pitch them.
What you want to see: The business appears in results with
accurate information pulled from the website.
Example searches to run:
  - "[business name] [city]"
  - "[service type] in [city]"
  - "best [service] near [city]"

CHATGPT — MANUAL CHECK
URL: chatgpt.com
Cost: Free
What it does: Same as above — directly tests AI search
visibility in the world's most-used AI tool.
When to use: Same as Perplexity — 2–4 weeks after launch.
Example prompt: "What do you know about [business name]
in [city], [state]?"
