The Practice

Where precision
meets artistry

With over a decade of advanced training and thousands of treatments performed, I founded Ofirelle on a singular belief: true aesthetic medicine requires both clinical excellence and an artistic eye. Every face is unique — every treatment should be too.

My approach is always natural-first. The goal is never to change you, but to refresh and restore — delivering results that are visible, but never obvious. You should look like yourself, only rested, refined, and radiant.
