Common Questions

How do I know if this work is right for me?

If you find yourself craving more presence, meaning, and ease in your everyday life — and you're ready to do some gentle but meaningful inner work — you're in the right place. We begin with a free clarity call so you can feel into whether this is a good fit before committing.

Do I need any prior experience with coaching or somatic work?

Not at all. Many of my most profound clients come in with zero background in any of this. I meet you exactly where you are — with no jargon, no pressure, and no expectation that you arrive already "healed." That's what the work is for.

Are sessions virtual or in-person?

All one-on-one sessions are held via Zoom, making this work accessible to women wherever you are in the world. Women's Circles are offered seasonally, both virtually and (occasionally) in person in California.

What is somatic work, exactly?

Somatic work means working with the body as much as the mind. Rather than only talking about your experience, we bring gentle awareness to physical sensations, breath, and movement — because the body holds a kind of wisdom that words alone can't always access.

Is there a payment plan option?

Yes — payment plans are available for both the Deep Dive and Full Alchemy containers. I believe this work should be accessible to women who are truly called to it, and I do my best to make that possible. Let's talk on your clarity call.
