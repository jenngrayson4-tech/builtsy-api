About Your Guide

Hello, I'm Sara

I'm a certified life coach and somatic practitioner based in California, supporting women across the world through life transitions, identity shifts, and the slow work of coming home to themselves.

This work isn't about fixing you — because you were never broken. It's about remembering what was always true, and building a life that reflects it. I bring warmth, depth, and a steady presence to every session.

Certified Somatic Practitioner ICF Certified Coach 10+ Years Experience 300+ Women Supported Trauma-Informed
