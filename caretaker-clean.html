About Claire

A Caregiver Who Shows Up for Your Family

I'm Claire Bennett, a Portland-based childcare and senior care provider with 11 years of professional experience. I've worked with families ranging from newborns just days old to elderly adults who need reliable daily companionship — and I bring the same level of attentiveness and heart to every age group.

My background includes early childhood education coursework, formal senior care training through Oregon DHS, and years of hands-on experience that no certification can fully capture. I believe great care is built on consistency, communication, and genuine trust between me and the families I serve.

Every family has a different rhythm. My job is to learn yours, respect it, and step in seamlessly whether you need me for a single afternoon or on a long-term recurring schedule.

  • Early Childhood Education coursework, Portland Community College
  • Oregon DHS Senior Care Training, Level II
  • American Red Cross CPR & First Aid Certified
  • Newborn care specialist training completed 2021
  • Fully insured, bonded, and background verified annually
