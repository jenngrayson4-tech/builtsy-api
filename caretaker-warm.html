A little about me

Raising children
is an honor

I'm Sophie Lane, a Montessori-trained nanny and childcare specialist with eight-plus years of experience supporting families across Brookside, Riverside, and Midtown. I believe every child deserves a safe, loving environment where curiosity is celebrated and independence is gently encouraged. From soothing a newborn to helping a six-year-old sound out their first chapter book, I show up fully present every single day. Families trust me not just because of my credentials, but because I genuinely love what I do.

What I offer

Care for every
stage of childhood

From the earliest newborn days through the busy after-school years, I tailor my approach to fit your child and your family.

Infant & Newborn Care

Specialized, gentle care for babies from birth through 12 months, with a strong focus on secure attachment, feeding support, and predictable sleep routines. I partner closely with parents during the most tender season of their baby's life.

Newborn Specialist Sleep Routines Feeding Support

Toddler & Preschool

Engaging, Montessori-inspired activities that nurture language, motor development, and social confidence in children ages one through five. Every day is filled with purposeful play, outdoor exploration, and age-appropriate independence.

Montessori Activities Outdoor Play Language Development

After School Care

Reliable pick-up, healthy snacks, homework help, and plenty of downtime for school-age children. I provide structure without rigidity so kids can decompress, recharge, and still feel accomplished before dinner.

School Pick-Up Homework Help Snack Prep

Overnight & Travel Care

Extended coverage for parents traveling for work or a long-awaited getaway. I maintain your children's routines, keep the household steady, and provide full peace of mind so you can be truly present wherever you are.
