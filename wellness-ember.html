About

A practice built on presence

I've spent over a decade studying the ancient and the modern — weaving together breathwork, somatic healing, and contemplative movement into sessions that meet you exactly where you are. My path began with a simple question: what does it feel like to truly inhabit your own body?

Every session I offer is an invitation — not to fix or force, but to slow down, listen inward, and return to yourself. Whether you're navigating burnout, seeking clarity, or simply craving stillness, this work will meet you there.
