About the Founder

I make brands impossible
to scroll past.

Hi, I'm Maya — the creative force behind Cherry Bomb Creative. With over seven years living inside the ever-shifting world of social media, I've built a practice rooted in one truth: authenticity sells. Not the watered-down, committee-approved kind — the kind that makes your audience feel like you're speaking directly to them.

Before going independent, I led social strategy for a boutique fashion house in New York, a Michelin-starred restaurant group, and a direct-to-consumer wellness brand that grew from 8K to 240K followers in fourteen months. What I discovered is that every brand has a magnetic story — most just haven't found the right voice to tell it.

I work with a select roster of clients at a time, because great strategy isn't a template — it's a conversation. If you're ready to stop blending in and start being unforgettable, let's talk.
