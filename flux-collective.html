Influencer Strategy

Our influencer strategy service is end-to-end — from identifying the right creators for your brand to managing relationships, briefing content, reviewing deliverables, and tracking every dollar of return. We work with macro, micro, and nano-influencers depending on your goals and budget.

Every campaign is built around a clear KPI framework: awareness, engagement, conversions, or all three. We don't just match you with influencers who "look right" — we build partnerships that perform.

  • Creator identification and vetting (10–50 creators per campaign)
  • Contract negotiation and relationship management
  • Full creative brief and content approval workflow
  • Real-time campaign reporting dashboard access
  • Post-campaign performance analysis and learnings report

FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Can't find your answer here? Send us a message.

What industries do you work with? +

We specialize in beauty, fashion, lifestyle, and wellness brands — categories where culture, community, and content intersect most powerfully. We've also worked with food & beverage, fitness, and DTC consumer brands with great results.

What's your minimum contract length? +

We require a 3-month minimum for all retainer engagements. Great social media strategy takes time to build, optimize, and compound — we've found 3 months is the minimum needed to show meaningful results. Most clients renew for 6–12 months.

Do you work with startups and emerging brands? +

Yes — some of our most exciting work has been with early-stage brands. If you're pre-revenue or pre-launch, our Starter Package is designed specifically for brands in the building phase. We love being part of something from the beginning.

How do you measure success? +

We define success around your actual business goals — revenue, community growth, engagement quality, influencer ROI, or brand awareness. Every client gets a custom KPI dashboard so you can see exactly what's working and how it connects to your bottom line.
