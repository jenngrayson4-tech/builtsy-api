About the Studio

Marlowe Studio was founded on the belief that every home deserves intention. We work with homeowners, developers, and real estate professionals to craft interiors that are as livable as they are beautiful — spaces with warmth in the walls and calm in the corners.

With over a decade of residential and staging experience, our work spans cozy bungalows to expansive estates. We listen before we design, and we believe the best rooms are the ones you never want to leave.

Based in Los Angeles, we serve clients locally and virtually nationwide.
