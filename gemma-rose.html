Your Questions, Answered

Do I need to have a finished manuscript to work with you?

Not at all. Whether you have a fully polished draft, a half-finished outline, or just a big burning idea you can't shake — we can work with it. In fact, some of the most transformative work happens in the early "what am I even making?" stage.

Is the Mastermind right for me if I'm just starting out?

The Mastermind is best suited for creatives who have already taken some steps toward their dream — maybe you've started creating content, have a manuscript in progress, or have launched a small coaching practice. You don't need to have it all figured out, but you should be ready to commit fully and show up for the work.

How many people are in the Mastermind?

The Mastermind is intentionally small — capped at 12 people per cohort. This allows for real connection, personalized attention, and the kind of intimate community where deep creative breakthroughs actually happen.

Is there a payment plan available?

Yes — payment plans are available for all coaching packages and the Mastermind. I believe that access to this kind of support shouldn't be limited to those who can pay in full upfront. We can work out a plan that makes sense for your situation.
