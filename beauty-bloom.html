About Me

Skin Care is
Self Care

Hi, I'm Sophia — a licensed esthetician with over 8 years of experience helping clients unlock their natural radiance. I believe every face tells a story, and every treatment should feel like a ritual. My studio is your sanctuary — a place to slow down, breathe deeply, and let your skin do the glowing.

I specialize in customized skincare protocols, precision brow artistry, and Hollywood lash extensions. Whether you're treating yourself or preparing for a milestone moment, I'm here to make you feel genuinely beautiful.
