Hi, I'm Mia

More than a sitter — a trusted part of your family.

I'm Mia Santos, a Chicago-based childcare provider with over a decade of hands-on experience caring for children from newborns to teenagers. I believe every child deserves an environment where they feel safe, celebrated, and endlessly curious. Whether it's building block towers at 8 AM or reading one more bedtime story at 8 PM, I show up fully — every single time.

I'm a graduate of the Early Childhood Education program at DePaul University, and I've worked with families across Lincoln Park, Bucktown, Wicker Park, and Logan Square. When I'm not caring for your little ones, you'll find me at the farmers market or teaching my rescue dog new tricks.
