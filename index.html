Platform Features

Everything
The Crew Needs.

Built for the 5–20 person contractor running real jobs — not the enterprise PM at a desk.

01

Jobs Dashboard

Live snapshot of every active job — status, value, client, dates. Know where everything stands without asking.

02

Daily Logs

Workers log hours, weather, notes, and materials from the field. Talk to the AI or tap through the form. Either way it's in the system.

03

Schedule + Gantt

Calendar view and timeline with a today-line. Schedule events through the AI or directly. Never miss an inspection again.

04

Change Orders

Draft, send, track approval. Cost and schedule impacts logged. Full draft → sent → approved workflow.

05

Punch List

End-of-project deficiency tracking with priority, assignment, and status. Close out clean.

06

Selections Tracker

Client allowance tracking for flooring, fixtures, and finishes. Budget vs. actual. No more spreadsheets.

07

Worker Management

Team roster, role-based access (admin vs. worker), job assignments. Your crew, your control.

08

Documents

Plans, permits, contracts, receipts — organized by category per job. Everything in one place.

09

RFIs

Chat-style requests sent to architects and clients via a shareable link. Responses log back to the job automatically.
