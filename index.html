✦ Our story
A mom built this for her 14-year-old son.
"My son told me he felt guilty
for how much AI he was using."

Grayson runs Rise Web Studio out of Parker, Colorado — building real client websites, charging real money, using AI to deliver professional work at 14 years old.

But he was hitting limits. Using prompt after prompt. The AI kept going in circles — and one day he told me he felt guilty for how much AI he was burning through just to build one site.

A 14-year-old already understood something most adults don't. More prompts isn't better. Structure is. That moment is the entire reason Builtsy Blueprints exists.

I built this accidentally. But completely intentionally.
No code. One person. Me.

Jennifer Lane
Founder of Builtsy · Non-technical developer · No code · Built it herself
